Project Overview

This project analyzes Zomato restaurant data to identify trends in restaurant distribution, pricing, customer preferences, ratings, cuisines, and market expansion opportunities.

The analysis combines Excel/Google Sheets techniques with business-focused exploratory analysis to support decisions around restaurant expansion, pricing, delivery, and customer engagement.

Dataset

The dataset contains two sheets:

Raw Data – detailed restaurant-level information
Country Description – mapping of country codes to country names

The raw data includes restaurant details, location, cuisines, price range, average cost for two, table booking, online delivery, votes, ratings, currency, and opening date.

Key Insights
India has the highest number of restaurants in the dataset, with 8,652 restaurants.
Countries such as Australia, Canada, Indonesia, Qatar, and Singapore show lower restaurant density and can represent potential expansion opportunities.
India has a large concentration of restaurants in lower price ranges, while some markets have stronger representation in higher price ranges.
Restaurant ratings can be influenced by factors such as perceived value, food quality, service quality, and customer expectations.
Online delivery and table booking can improve convenience and accessibility, but service quality and operational execution can affect customer ratings.
Customer votes and ratings provide useful indicators of customer engagement and restaurant performance.
Business Recommendations
Explore lower-competition international markets for restaurant expansion.
Identify cities with lower restaurant density before opening new locations.
Focus on popular cuisines while adapting offerings to local preferences.
Strengthen online delivery infrastructure in markets with growth potential.
Use customer ratings and reviews to monitor service quality.
Apply market-specific pricing strategies based on customer price sensitivity and competition.
Maintain consistent food quality, packaging, delivery operations, and customer service.

Conclusion

The analysis highlights significant differences in restaurant density, pricing, cuisines, and service availability across countries. India is the largest market in the dataset, while several countries with lower restaurant density present opportunities for expansion.

Data-driven decisions around location, cuisine, pricing, delivery, and customer engagement can help support sustainable restaurant growth.
